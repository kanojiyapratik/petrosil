Price rules

Most locations are never priced in the market reports. Their price is worked out from one that is — for example South Korea = Singapore − 10%. Set those sums here. The system does the arithmetic, not the AI, and every price it works out is shown for you to check before anything is saved to the website.

Rounding

Every price offered to you on the editorial screen — read straight from a report, converted from another currency, or worked out above — rounds to this step before you see it. Leave it blank for no rounding.

In each price's own currency: 5 means $5 on a dollar price, €5 on a euro price and ₹5 on a rupee price.
