Price rules

Many locations never appear in the market reports — their price is always calculated from a benchmark, e.g. South Korea = Singapore − 10%. The rules live here, the calculation is done by the system (never the AI), and calculated prices appear on the draft marked derived, for review like any other. Change a percentage here and the next draft uses it.
