Sites

Each site is reached over its own API with its own token. Adding one here is all it takes — no redeploy. Show and Hide control who can use a site: a hidden site is missing from the site list and its screens answer “no such site”, so a site being set up cannot be reached by anyone who guesses the address. Every change is recorded against your name.
